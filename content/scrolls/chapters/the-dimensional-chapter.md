>- **Status**: Pre-Planning Stage
>- **Protagonist**: Yūzu Hiiragi
>- **Author Notes**: A full rewrite of Arc-V. The largest retelling on scope by far. The core elements will stay (a dimensional war, the bracelet girls, the Zarc-Rei plot) but there will be a ton of changes. Major differences will include having Yuzu as the main protagonist instead of Yuya; having no legacy characters; having a more focused plan for each dimension; and having a compelling final arc and resolution.
