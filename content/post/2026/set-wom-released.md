**World of Memories** is the new set released in Kingdom Duels on 2026-10-26.

#### Breakdown

- Codename: **[WOM]**
- It has **230 cards** from episodes [124 to 140](/story/world-of-memories-arc/) of the Yugioh Scrolls project.
- It is focused on the adaptation of the "Pharaoh Memories" arc but with a lot of changes and weird rules to make it so that the whole narrative can be represented with the Kingdom Duels cardgame. This is the final set release of the "Millennium Chapter" cards, and also includes the "Ceremonial Duel" adaptation. Iconic cards like Zorc, Horakhty and Silent Magician make their debut here.
- This set provides support for the following duelists: Seto, Akhenaden, Shada, Karim, Seto, Isis, Mahado, The Pharaoh, Thief King Bakura, Kisara, Suspects, Gebelk, Mana, Shadow Riders, Hasan, Bakura, Kaiba, Honda, Anzu, Jonouchi, Yugi, Atem. 
- [**CARD GALLERY**](/cards/?search=set%253AWOM)

#### Additional Notes

- Several older cards from all sets have been updated with updated images or with minor fixes for wording, effects or stats that needed adjustment.
- As previously mentioned, this will be the last card set in a long time, as i will take a break and focus on [pending changes, the story, and the simulator](/post/website-new-design/).
