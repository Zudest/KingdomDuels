The deckbuilder has been rebuilt from the ground up and it is now the [**Card Library**](/cards/). It kept everything it did before but the way you browse, filter and create your decks has changed quite a bit.

Note: It also was moved to a new address: **[kingdomduels.com/cards](/cards/)**. The old `/deckbuilder/` links still work and will redirect you though.

#### Layout

- The old grid showed 8 cards at a time on a 1080p screen, with the deck panel eating a third of the width. Now the filters and the deckbuilder share a single sidebar with two tabs, which leaves the rest of the page for the cards.
- 6 or 10 columns for cards (there is a **S / L** toggle in the toolbar).
- Cards already in your deck are highlighted in the grid.
- Filters, deck counters and pagination stay visible while you scroll. There are small counters on the sidebar for the deck progress too.
- Cards in the deckbuild are showing only as text so they are better presented without unnecessary scrolling, and have a hover with card information and a bigger image for quick reference.
- Rows in the deck have **−** and **+** buttons, so changing from three copies to two no longer means deleting the card and finding it again.
- Just as before: **left click** adds a card to the corresponding deck, **right click** shows it enlarged.

#### Searching

There is now a single search bar at the top, and typing a plain name just works. The scryfall-style syntax is still there if you want it, and the `?` button next to the bar lists every field.

- Filters apply as you type, so the "Apply Filters" button is gone. Whatever you filter shows up as removable green chips above the grid.
- Every dropdown is now searchable and tells you how many cards each option has. This includes the new **duelist** and **types** dropdown.
- ATK, DEF, Bonus ATK and Bonus DEF now have visible **>= > <= < !=** operators. Those things always worked, but you had to know the syntax to use them.
- **Grade** and its value work as a pair: pick a Grade and then the value control adapts to it (for example picking Level shows a numeric control while Glyph shows a dropdown of options).
- Alternative artworks are now combined with the base ones so they are easier to find, browse and add to your deck.

#### Effect Search

Something that was requested for a while: You can now search the actual **effect text** of every card (finally!, lol)
